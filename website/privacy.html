Privacy Policy — YouTube-SuperUploaderMCP

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Who this app is for

YouTube-SuperUploaderMCP is a private tool used only by its owner, S M Abdul Mueid, to upload videos to 9 YouTube channels they manage. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

Through the YouTube Data API v3, the app accesses only what is needed to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels:

  • YouTube channel identity (channel ID, title, handle) — to confirm uploads go to the right channel.
  • Video metadata the owner provides (title, description, tags, thumbnail).
  • Upload status and YouTube Analytics read-only data for the owner's channels.

The app does not access email, contacts, files, location, or any other Google service.

How OAuth tokens are stored

Each channel is connected once with Google OAuth. The resulting refresh token is stored only on the owner's own machine, in a file with restricted permissions, and is used only to upload to that one channel. Tokens are never copied to any other system, never shown in logs or messages, and never shared with anyone.

Sharing and selling

The app does not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third party, for any purpose. Data is sent only to Google's YouTube API to perform the upload or check you asked for.

Google API Limited Use disclosure

YouTube-SuperUploaderMCP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Upload logs (video title, video ID, upload time, channel) are kept on the owner's machine for record-keeping. You can delete the app's stored tokens at any time by deleting the token files, or by revoking access (below). Deleting a token stops all future uploads for that channel; already-published videos are unaffected and must be removed from YouTube itself if wanted.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (remove "YouTube-SuperUploaderMCP" — listed as "Nevo YouTube Uploader" on Google's consent screen until the app name is updated there). Revoking immediately stops the app from accessing that Google account.
